Projects

  • Visualizing Bentley's University water usage

    This project visualized Bentley University's inconsistent water usage data for the Office of Sustainability. The solution was an accessible dashboard, "Plastic Bottles Avoided," with a colorblind-friendly palette and clear layout. The dashboard helps staff identify trends, track progress, and locate problematic water stations.

  • Tomato Dice

    The "Tomato Dice" is a multimodal device that helps remote workers take effective breaks through timeboxing with visual and audio cues. After a year of ideation and prototyping, a usability study found users engaged with the dice and preferred it for relaxation. Recommendations included customization, volume control, and a silent mode for shared workspaces.
